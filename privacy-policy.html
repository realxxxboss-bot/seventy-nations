Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Seventy Nations (“we,” “us,” “our”) — The Seventy Nations (R.A.) Jerusalem, a registered Israeli nonprofit association, and its sister organization, The Jerusalem Unity Center USA, a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit foundation — collects, uses, and protects information from visitors to theseventynations.org (the “Site”) and participants in our programs.

By using the Site or engaging with our programs, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

At a Glance

What We Collect

What you choose to share when you donate, join, register, or write to us — plus basic, general usage data from the Site.

How We Use It

To process donations and applications, register you for events and courses, and send only the updates you have opted in to.

How We Protect It

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, and trusted third-party providers. We do not sell your personal information.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe at any time, and request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information we hold about you.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly, such as when you:

  • Make a donation
  • Become a member or join our global community
  • Register as a Volunteer or Ambassador
  • Apply to start or join a Global Chapter
  • Register for an Event, conference, workshop, or retreat
  • Enroll in a Learn course, pathway, or Institute program
  • Sign up for our newsletter or contact us with an inquiry
  • Participate in Research initiatives or surveys we conduct

This may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, country/chapter location, payment information (processed securely by our third-party payment processor — we do not store full payment card details), and any additional details you choose to share with us.

Information collected automatically, such as when you browse the Site:

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and general location (city/country level)
  • Pages visited, time spent on the Site, and referring website or search terms
  • Cookie and similar tracking data (see Section 4 below)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process donations and issue tax-deductible donation receipts (through The Jerusalem Unity Center USA, where applicable)
  • Process membership, volunteer, ambassador, and chapter applications
  • Register you for events, courses, and research programs, and communicate related details
  • Send newsletters, program updates, or event announcements (only if you’ve opted in)
  • Connect you with the appropriate Global Chapter or community group based on your location
  • Improve our Site, programs, and Institutes based on how visitors and members engage with them
  • Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations related to donations in Israel and the United States

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To operate the Site and our programs, we rely on trusted third-party service providers, which may process your information on our behalf:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — to understand how visitors use our Site (pages viewed, general location, referral source). Learn how Google handles data.
  • Payment/donation processor Donorbox — to securely process donations. We do not directly store your full payment details.
  • Event/course registration platform (where applicable) — to manage Event and Learn program sign-ups.
  • Email/newsletter provider Klaviyo — to send newsletters and updates to subscribers who opt in.
  • Website hosting and content management platform Bluehost — to host and operate the Site itself.

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review them.

4. Cookies

Our Site uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how visitors use the Site (via Google Analytics)
  • Support donation, membership, and event registration functionality

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some Site functionality, such as donation or registration forms.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

1

You Share

You donate, register, join, or write to us — sharing only what you choose to share.

You may access, correct, or delete it, and unsubscribe from our emails, at any time.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may unsubscribe from our newsletter or email communications at any time using the link provided in each email.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at info@theseventynations.org.
  • Depending on your country of residence, you may have additional rights under applicable law (such as the GDPR for visitors in the European Union, or Israeli data protection law).

7. Children’s Privacy

Our Site and programs are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. International Visitors, Members, and Chapters

The Seventy Nations operates across two entities — The Seventy Nations (R.A.) Jerusalem in Israel and The Jerusalem Unity Center USA — and welcomes members, donors, volunteers, and Global Chapters around the world. By using our Site or joining a Chapter, you understand that your information may be processed in Israel, the United States, and other countries where our service providers or local Chapters operate, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
