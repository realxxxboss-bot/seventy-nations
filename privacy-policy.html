Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The Seventy Nations Jerusalem Foundation is built on the values of human dignity, moral responsibility, and trust. That same respect guides how we treat your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have — in plain language, without hidden clauses.

This policy applies to theseventynations.org and the online forms, newsletters, and donation activities we offer through it.

At a Glance

What We Collect

Only what you choose to share — your name, email, and the details you enter in our forms — plus basic, anonymous usage data.

How We Use It

To respond to your inquiries, share updates you ask for, process donations, and improve the experience of our community.

How We Protect It

We use reasonable safeguards, trusted service providers, and spam protection. We never sell your information.

Your Choices

You can access, correct, or delete your details, and unsubscribe from emails at any time. Just contact us.

Information We Collect

We collect two kinds of information:

Information you provide to us

When you complete our “Join the Movement” form, subscribe to our newsletter, make a donation, or otherwise contact us, you may share:

  • Your first and last name;
  • Your email address, and optionally your phone number;
  • Your country and, if you provide it, your organization;
  • Your area of interest and any message you write to us;
  • Donation details, which are processed securely by our third-party payment partners.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we may collect limited, non-identifying technical information — such as your browser type, device, general location, and the pages you visit — through cookies and standard analytics tools. This helps us understand how the site is used and how to improve it.

How Your Information Moves

1

You Share

You submit a form, subscribe, or make a donation of your own choosing.

2

We Receive

Your information reaches our team securely and is stored so your inquiry is never lost.

3

We Respond

We use it only to reply, keep you informed, and support the purpose you contacted us for.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests;
  • Send you updates, event announcements, and programs — when you have asked to receive them;
  • Process and acknowledge donations and issue receipts where applicable;
  • Explore partnership, volunteer, and collaboration opportunities you express interest in;
  • Maintain, secure, and improve our website and communications;
  • Comply with our legal and reporting obligations as a nonprofit organization.

Cookies & Analytics

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help a website function and remember preferences. We use only essential and analytics cookies to keep the site working and to understand general usage. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work as intended if you do.

How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share it only in these limited situations:

  • Trusted service providers who help us operate — such as email, form-handling, analytics, and payment-processing partners — and only to perform those services on our behalf;
  • Our affiliated organizations, including The Jerusalem Unity Center USA, where necessary to fulfill your request or process a tax-deductible donation;
  • Legal requirements, when we are obligated to comply with the law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our community and organization.

Donations

Donations are handled by secure, third-party payment processors; we do not store your full payment-card details on our website. The Seventy Nations (R.A.) Jerusalem is a registered Israeli nonprofit association that partners with its sister organization, The Jerusalem Unity Center USA, a 501(c) nonprofit foundation through which donors may be eligible for tax deductions. Information related to your donation is used to process it, acknowledge it, and meet applicable reporting requirements.

How We Protect Your Information

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to safeguard your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. Our contact form includes spam protection, and access to submissions is limited to those who need it. No method of transmission over the internet is ever completely secure, but we work diligently to protect what you entrust to us.

Your Rights & Choices

You remain in control of your information. At any time you may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information;
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to our legal obligations;
  • Unsubscribe from our emails using the link in any message, or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these choices, please email us at info@theseventynations.org.

International Community

The Seventy Nations is based in Jerusalem, Israel, and serves a global community. If you contact us from outside Israel, your information may be processed in Israel or in the countries where our service providers operate. By using our website, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in these locations, always in accordance with this policy.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

Third-Party Links

Our website may link to other websites and initiatives, including the Jerusalem Unity Center and partner organizations. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites, and we encourage you to review their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, we would be glad to hear from you.
